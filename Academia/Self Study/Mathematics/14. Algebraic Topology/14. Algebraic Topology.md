## Overview

**Algebraic Topology** translates topological problems into algebraic ones by assigning algebraic invariants (groups, rings, modules) to topological spaces such that homeomorphic spaces have isomorphic algebraic invariants.

## Standard Topological Invariants

- **Fundamental Group** $\pi_1(X)$: Detects 1-dimensional holes / loops.
- **Higher Homotopy Groups** $\pi_n(X)$: Detects $n$-dimensional spheres $S^n \to X$.
- **Homology Groups** $H_n(X)$: Measures $n$-dimensional cycles modulo boundaries.
