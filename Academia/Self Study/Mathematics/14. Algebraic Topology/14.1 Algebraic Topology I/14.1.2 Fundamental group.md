## Definition

Let $X$ be a topological space and $x_0 \in X$ be a basepoint.

A **loop** in $X$ based at $x_0$ is a continuous map $\gamma : [0, 1] \to X$ such that $\gamma(0) = \gamma(1) = x_0$.

The **fundamental group** $\pi_1(X, x_0)$ is the set of homotopy classes $[\gamma]$ of loops based at $x_0$ under path concatenation relative to endpoints.

---

## Group Operations on Loops

Let $f, g : [0, 1] \to X$ be loops based at $x_0$.

| Operation | Formula | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Concatenation** | $(f * g)(t) = \begin{cases} f(2t) & 0 \le t \le 1/2 \\ g(2t - 1) & 1/2 \le t \le 1 \end{cases}$ | Traverse $f$ at double speed, then traverse $g$. |
| **Identity** | $c_{x_0}(t) = x_0$ | Constant loop at $x_0$. |
| **Inverse** | $\bar{f}(t) = f(1 - t)$ | Traverse loop $f$ in reverse direction. |

## Basepoint Independence & Simple Connectivity

### Basepoint Independence

If $X$ is path-connected, then $\pi_1(X, x_0) \cong \pi_1(X, x_1)$ for any $x_0, x_1 \in X$. We often write simply $\pi_1(X)$.

### Simply Connected Space

A path-connected space $X$ is **simply connected** if $\pi_1(X) \cong \{0\}$ (trivial group).

> [!example] Fundamental Groups of Classical Spaces
> - $\pi_1(\mathbb{R}^n) \cong \{0\}$ (simply connected for all $n$)
> - $\pi_1(S^1) \cong \mathbb{Z}$ (circle)
> - $\pi_1(S^n) \cong \{0\}$ for $n \ge 2$ (higher dimensional spheres)
> - $\pi_1(S^1 \times S^1) \cong \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}$ (torus $T^2$)
> - $\pi_1(\mathbb{R}P^2) \cong \mathbb{Z}_2$ (real projective plane)

## Practice Problems

1. Prove that fundamental group is a topological invariant: if $X \cong Y$, then $\pi_1(X) \cong \pi_1(Y)$.
2. Show that if $X$ is contractible, then $\pi_1(X) \cong \{0\}$.
3. Compute the fundamental group of the figure-eight space $S^1 \vee S^1$ (wedge sum of two circles).
4. State Van Kampen's Theorem and use it to compute $\pi_1(S^2)$.
5. Calculate $\pi_1(T^2)$ where $T^2 = S^1 \times S^1$ is the 2-torus.
