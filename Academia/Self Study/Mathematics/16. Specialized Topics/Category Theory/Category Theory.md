## Overview

Category theory studies mathematical objects through the structure-preserving maps between them. It is a common language for algebra, topology, logic, and computer science.

## Key Concepts

A category has objects, morphisms, identities, and associative composition. A functor maps objects and morphisms while preserving identities and composition. Universal properties characterize constructions uniquely up to unique isomorphism.

## Major Results

Yoneda's lemma identifies an object by the morphisms into or out of it. Adjunctions generalize free constructions, and limits and colimits include products, equalizers, pullbacks, coproducts, and coequalizers.

## Practice Problems
