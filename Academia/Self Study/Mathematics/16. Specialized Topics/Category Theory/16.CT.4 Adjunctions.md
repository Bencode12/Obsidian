## Definition

An adjunction $F\dashv G$ is a natural bijection $\operatorname{Hom}_D(FX,Y)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_C(X,GY)$. $F$ is left adjoint and $G$ is right adjoint.

## Examples

The free-group functor is left adjoint to the forgetful functor from groups to sets. Products are right adjoint to diagonal functors, while coproducts are left adjoint.

## Practice Problems

1. Identify the unit and counit of an adjunction.
2. Verify the free-forgetful hom-set bijection.
3. Explain why adjoints preserve colimits or limits respectively.
