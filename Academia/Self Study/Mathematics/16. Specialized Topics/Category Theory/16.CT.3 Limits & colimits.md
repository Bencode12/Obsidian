## Definition

A limit of a diagram is a universal cone into that diagram; a colimit is a universal cocone out of it. Universality means every other cone or cocone factors uniquely through the chosen one.

## Examples

Products, equalizers, and pullbacks are limits. Coproducts, coequalizers, and pushouts are colimits. In sets, a product is the set of tuples with one coordinate for each factor.

## Practice Problems

1. State the universal property of a product.
2. Describe a pullback of two functions of sets.
3. Explain why a limit, when it exists, is unique up to unique isomorphism.
