## Definition

A monad on $C$ is a functor $T:C\to C$ with natural transformations $\eta:1_C\Rightarrow T$ and $\mu:T^2\Rightarrow T$ satisfying unit and associativity laws. It packages repeated structure into one composable operation.

## Examples

The list monad sends a set to its finite lists; the identity monad adds no structure. In programming, monads provide a disciplined way to sequence computations with context or effects.

## Practice Problems

1. State the two unit laws and associativity law for a monad.
2. Describe the list monad's multiplication by flattening lists of lists.
3. Explain how every adjunction produces a monad.
