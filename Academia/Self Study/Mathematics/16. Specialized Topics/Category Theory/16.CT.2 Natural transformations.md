## Definition

Given functors $F,G:C\to D$, a natural transformation $\eta:F\Rightarrow G$ assigns each object $X$ a morphism $\eta_X:F(X)\to G(X)$ such that $G(f)\circ\eta_X=\eta_Y\circ F(f)$ for every $f:X\to Y$.

## Key Idea

The square formed by every morphism must commute. A natural isomorphism has each component invertible and expresses equivalence that respects all maps in the category.

## Practice Problems

1. Write the naturality square for a morphism $f:X\to Y$.
2. Find a natural transformation between two familiar functors.
3. Explain why componentwise maps alone are not enough for naturality.
