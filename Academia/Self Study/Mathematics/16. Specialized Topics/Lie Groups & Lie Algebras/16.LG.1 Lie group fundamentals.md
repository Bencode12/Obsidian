## Definition

A Lie group is a smooth manifold $G$ whose multiplication and inversion maps are smooth. The tangent space $T_eG$ at the identity becomes a Lie algebra through left-invariant vector fields.

## Examples

$\mathbb{R}^n$ under addition is abelian. Matrix groups such as $GL_n(\mathbb{R})$, $SL_n(\mathbb{R})$, and $SO(n)$ are Lie groups.

## Practice Problems

1. Verify that matrix multiplication makes $GL_n(\mathbb{R})$ a Lie group.
2. Find the dimension of $SO(3)$.
3. Compute the bracket of two left-invariant vector fields.
