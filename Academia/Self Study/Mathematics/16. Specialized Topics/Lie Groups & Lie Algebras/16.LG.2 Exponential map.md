## Definition

For a matrix Lie group, the exponential map is $\exp(A)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}A^k/k!$. It maps a Lie algebra element to the one-parameter subgroup $t\mapsto\exp(tA)$.

## Key Properties

$\exp(0)=I$ and $\frac{d}{dt}\exp(tA)=A\exp(tA)$. If $A$ and $B$ commute, then $\exp(A+B)=\exp(A)\exp(B)$; this need not hold otherwise.

## Practice Problems

1. Compute the exponential of a diagonal matrix.
2. Show that $t\mapsto\exp(tA)$ satisfies a matrix differential equation.
3. Give an example where $AB\ne BA$ and the exponential product rule fails.
