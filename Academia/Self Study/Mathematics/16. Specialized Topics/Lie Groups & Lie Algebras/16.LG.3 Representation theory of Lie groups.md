## Definition

A representation of a group $G$ on a vector space $V$ is a homomorphism $\rho:G\to GL(V)$. A representation is irreducible when it has no nonzero proper invariant subspaces.

## Key Idea

Representations turn symmetry into linear algebra. For compact groups, finite-dimensional representations decompose into irreducibles under suitable inner products; characters record traces and help distinguish them.
