## Overview

Ergodic theory studies long-term behavior of measure-preserving dynamical systems and explains when time averages agree with space averages.

## Key Concepts

A measure-preserving system is $(X,\mathcal{B},\mu,T)$ with $\mu(T^{-1}A)=\mu(A)$. It is ergodic when every invariant measurable set has measure $0$ or $1. Mixing is stronger: $\mu(T^{-n}A\cap B)\to\mu(A)\mu(B)$.

## Major Results

Birkhoff's ergodic theorem gives almost-everywhere convergence of time averages. The von Neumann mean ergodic theorem gives convergence in $L^2$. Poincare recurrence guarantees recurrent points in finite-measure systems.

4. State Birkhoff's theorem and identify its hypotheses.
