## Suggested Prerequisites

> [!info] Prerequisites
> [[12. Analysis]] supports ergodic theory, PDEs, operator algebras, and spectral theory. [[15. Abstract Algebra]] supports category theory, homological algebra, and Lie theory. [[9. Linear Algebra]] is useful throughout.

## Study Method

For each branch, define the objects first, work through a concrete example, state the main theorem with its hypotheses, and record how the topic connects to the core notes.

## References

- The specialized-topic notes contain focused references for each branch.
- The most reliable progression is to pair a textbook with exercises and a proof-oriented prerequisite review.
