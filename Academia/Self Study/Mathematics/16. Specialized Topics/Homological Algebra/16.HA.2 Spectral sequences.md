## Definition

A spectral sequence is a sequence of pages $(E_r,d_r)$ whose successive homology groups define the next page: $E_{r+1}=H(E_r,d_r)$. Under convergence hypotheses, it approaches a graded object associated with the desired invariant.

## Key Idea

A filtration breaks a hard calculation into layers. Differentials remove classes that cannot survive, and the stable page often computes homology, cohomology, or an associated graded object.

## Practice Problems

1. Explain what it means for a spectral sequence to converge.
2. Identify the role of the differential $d_r$.
3. Describe how a filtration produces an associated graded object.
