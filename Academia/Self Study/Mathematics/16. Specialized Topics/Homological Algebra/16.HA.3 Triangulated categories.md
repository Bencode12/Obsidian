## Definition

A triangulated category has an additive structure, a shift functor $[1]$, and distinguished triangles $X\to Y\to Z\to X[1]$ satisfying axioms that formalize exact-sequence behavior.

## Key Idea

Triangles encode cones and long exact sequences in homology. Derived categories are fundamental examples, although modern work often refines them using stable infinity-categories.

## Practice Problems

1. Write the long exact sequence induced by a short exact sequence of complexes.
2. Explain the role of the shift $X[1]$.
3. Describe a mapping cone of a chain map.
