## Definition

The derived category of an abelian category is formed from chain complexes by identifying chain-homotopy-equivalent information and inverting quasi-isomorphisms, maps inducing isomorphisms on homology.

## Key Idea

Derived categories make resolutions independent of the chosen model. Derived functors such as $\operatorname{Ext}$ and $\operatorname{Tor}$ record how ordinary functors fail to preserve exact sequences.
