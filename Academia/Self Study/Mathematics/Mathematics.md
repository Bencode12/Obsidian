## Mathematics Self-Study

This vault is organized as a progression from foundational reasoning through core undergraduate mathematics and into specialized subjects.

> [!info] Suggested route
> Begin with [[0. Foundational Thinking]], then use [[1. Arithmetic]], [[2. Pre-Algebra]], [[3. Algebra]], and [[4. Geometry]] before moving to [[5. Advanced Algebra & Trigonometry]] and [[6. Calculus]]. Linear algebra, probability, statistics, analysis, topology, algebraic topology, abstract algebra, number theory, logic, and combinatorics can then be studied in parallel.

## Study Practice

For each topic, define the objects, work through examples, prove central results, and finish the practice problems without looking at the solution first. Link concepts across folders when the same idea appears in a new setting.
