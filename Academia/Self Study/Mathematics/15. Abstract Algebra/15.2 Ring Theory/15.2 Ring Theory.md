## Overview

**Ring Theory** explores algebraic structures equipped with two binary operations (addition $+$ and multiplication $\cdot$), generalizing number systems like $\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Q}, \mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C}$ as well as polynomial systems and matrix rings.
