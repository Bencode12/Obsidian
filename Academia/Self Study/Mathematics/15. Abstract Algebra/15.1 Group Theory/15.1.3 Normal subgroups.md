## Definition

A subgroup $N \le G$ is called a **normal subgroup** (denoted $N \triangleleft G$) if its left and right cosets coincide for every element in $G$:

$$gN = Ng \quad \forall g \in G$$

Equivalent definitions of normality include:

| Condition | Formulate |
|---|---|
| **Conjugation Invariance** | $\forall g \in G, \; g N g^{-1} \subseteq N$ |
| **Set Conjugation Equality** | $\forall g \in G, \; g N g^{-1} = N$ |
| **Coset Equality** | $\forall g \in G, \; gN = Ng$ |

---

## Quotient Groups (Factor Groups)

If $N \triangleleft G$, the set of cosets $G/N = \{gN \mid g \in G\}$ forms a group under coset multiplication:

$$(aN)(bN) = (ab)N$$

> [!info] Why Normality is Necessary
> Coset multiplication $(aN)(bN) = (ab)N$ is **well-defined** if and only if $N$ is normal in $G$. If $N$ is not normal, the product depends on the choice of coset representatives!

### Properties of $G/N$

- **Identity element**: $eN = N$
- **Inverse element**: $(gN)^{-1} = g^{-1}N$
- **Order of quotient group**: $|G/N| = [G : N] = \frac{|G|}{|N|}$ (for finite $G$)

## Homomorphisms & Isomorphism Theorems

A function $\phi : G \to H$ between two groups is a **group homomorphism** if $\phi(ab) = \phi(a)\phi(b)$ for all $a, b \in G$.

- **Kernel**: $\ker(\phi) = \{g \in G \mid \phi(g) = e_H\}$.  
  *Key Theorem*: The kernel $\ker(\phi)$ is **always** a normal subgroup of $G$!
- **Image**: $\text{im}(\phi) = \{\phi(g) \mid g \in G\} \le H$.

### First Isomorphism Theorem

> [!example] First Isomorphism Theorem
> Let $\phi : G \to H$ be a group homomorphism. Then:
> $$G / \ker(\phi) \cong \text{im}(\phi)$$

### Second & Third Isomorphism Theorems

- **Second (Diamond) Isomorphism Theorem**: If $H \le G$ and $N \triangleleft G$, then $(HN)/N \cong H / (H \cap N)$.
- **Third Isomorphism Theorem**: If $N \triangleleft G$, $M \triangleleft G$, and $N \subseteq M$, then $(G/N)/(M/N) \cong G/M$.

## Common Mistakes

> [!warning] Normality is Not Transitive!
> If $K \triangleleft H$ and $H \triangleleft G$, it does **NOT** necessarily follow that $K \triangleleft G$.

## Practice Problems

1. Show that every subgroup of an abelian group $G$ is normal.
2. Prove that any subgroup $H \le G$ of index $[G : H] = 2$ is normal in $G$.
3. Let $\phi : G \to H$ be a group homomorphism. Prove that $\ker(\phi) \triangleleft G$.
4. Calculate the quotient group $\mathbb{Z} / n\mathbb{Z}$ and state its order.
5. Use the First Isomorphism Theorem to show that $\mathbb{R} / \mathbb{Z} \cong S^1$ (where $S^1$ is the circle group under multiplication $\{e^{i\theta} \mid \theta \in \mathbb{R}\}$).
